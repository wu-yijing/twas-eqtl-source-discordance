Figure S6. Endpoint calibration and positive control of the FDR enrichment endpoint. Panel (a) calibrates the detection boundary and single-gene power of the endpoint, plotted against the realised stratum size: the Benjamini–Hochberg detection boundary |Z| is read on the left axis and single-gene power (k = 1) on the right axis. Panel (b) gives the endpoint’s power to separate candidates from housekeeping controls as a function of the true rate difference. Numerical values, including the detection boundary at each realised stratum size and the smallest difference detectable at 80% power, are given in Additional file 1: Table S19; the observed pooled difference of +2.7 percentage points lies below the 80%-power boundary in both arms.
