Fig. 7. Cross-cohort replicability of a source-stable finding (RNH1). (a) RNH1 S-PrediXcan Z-scores using GTEx v8 Nerve_Tibial and eQTLGen across DR, DN and DPN. (b) Random-effects meta-analysis of RNH1 in DR using eQTLGen weights in both cohorts, computed with the unmodified official MetaXcan v0.8.1 binary (FinnGen R13 Z = +2.31, N_e = 49,304; UK Biobank GCST90043640 Z = +0.72, N_e = 1,231): Q = 1.26, P = 0.26; I² = 20.5%; τ = 0.51; pooled mean Z = +1.52 (SE 0.79; test P = 0.056); 95% prediction interval [34] −0.33 to +3.36 (orange), spanning zero. The √N_e-weighted re-merge gives pooled Z = +2.39 (P = 0.017) with Q = 0.13 and I² = 0%. Both cohorts are direction-consistent; the UK Biobank estimate is based on 308 cases and is therefore markedly less precise than the FinnGen estimate, and the prediction interval spanning zero means the direction cannot be reliably predicted in a new cohort. With k = 2 cohorts the heterogeneity statistics and prediction interval are descriptive only, and the between-cohort precision contrast is the primary evidence; the pooled Z is an unweighted mean of the two cohort Z-scores (Methods). N_e, effective sample size; Q, Cochran's heterogeneity statistic; τ, between-study standard deviation; PI, prediction interval.
