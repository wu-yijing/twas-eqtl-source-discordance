Fig. 8. Cross-trait generalisation of the within-GTEx tissue-context comparison (Whole_Blood vs Nerve_Tibial) across three gene sets. The HOTAIR-testbed and housekeeping points use FinnGen R13 GWAS data; the PGC3 SCZ estimate uses the PGC3 schizophrenia dataset and was recomputed with the official MetaXcan v0.8.1 binary. Points are Spearman’s ρ with Fisher-z 95% confidence intervals; n is the number of gene–phenotype comparisons. The three gene sets rest on different gene universes: 46 testbed genes with valid Z-scores in both GTEx tissues (ρ = +0.41, n = 138, 95% CI 0.27–0.54; the tissue-only arm of Table 3b), 24 housekeeping genes with both-tissue Z-scores (ρ = +0.64, n = 72, 95% CI 0.47–0.76), and a genome-wide PGC3 SCZ estimate (ρ = +0.42, n = 8,890, 95% CI 0.40–0.44). Two denominators are used for the SCZ estimate: the plotted estimate rests on every gene with a Z-score in both GTEx tissues (n = 8,890), whereas the four-arm decomposition requires a Z-score in both eQTLGen arms and rests on the n = 8,315 complete-case subset; the two subsets give the same correlation to three decimals (ρ = 0.420). SCZ, schizophrenia; PGC, Psychiatric Genomics Consortium.
