Figure S1. Exploratory diagnostic scheme for dual-source TWAS sensitivity analysis. The flowchart guides analysts from a single-source TWAS through source comparison and cross-cohort replication, with direction-consistency branch points (>75%, 60–75%, <60%) aligned with cut-points derived from the HOTAIR testbed distribution and externally calibrated against the genome-wide schizophrenia benchmark (PGC3 SCZ). The framework does not require the two axes to be statistically separable; it is anchored in direction consistency alone.
