Fig. 5. Gene-level eQTL weight-source discordance across 11 DR candidate genes. Paired bars show S-PrediXcan Z-scores for the 11 DR candidate genes with the largest |Z| under eQTLGen weights, derived from GTEx v8 Nerve_Tibial (red) compared with eQTLGen whole-blood (blue); YWHAQ carries no GTEx Nerve_Tibial model and is shown for eQTLGen only; the number of eQTLGen model SNPs retained after three-way harmonisation is tabulated per gene in Additional file 1: Table S17 (TUBB: 359 of 1,848 retained). All Z-scores are from the official MetaXcan v0.8.1 recompute. The largest TUBB eQTLGen Z-score in DR (+11.9) is included in all reported statistics, and the sensitivity of the FDR-significant count to this gene is given in Additional file 1: Table S21.
