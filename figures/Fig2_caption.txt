Fig. 2. Covariate balance before and after Mahalanobis matching. This panel displays standardised mean differences (SMD) for three matching covariates—log10-transformed gene length, GC content (%) and eQTL SNP count—comparing 30 candidate genes (red circles) and the 44 high-confidence non-candidate genes they were drawn from, all before matching, with the 30 matched pairs after Mahalanobis matching (blue squares); the dashed vertical lines mark the |SMD| < 0.25 adequacy threshold. Before matching the SMDs were −0.456 (gene length), −0.123 (eQTL SNP count) and +0.020 (GC content); after matching all three fell within the threshold (−0.143, +0.081 and +0.089 respectively). Missing eQTL SNP counts were imputed at the median within group in accordance with the pre-specified protocol. SMD, standardised mean differences.
