Figure S4. eQTL SNP count distribution across gene groups. Violin plots with individual data points show the number of eQTL SNPs in the eQTLGen S-PrediXcan predictive model, stratified by candidate (24 genes), non-candidate (24 genes) and T2DM control (13 genes) groups; the median count is annotated for each group (374, 632 and 669 SNPs respectively; overall median 512, IQR 215–901). The 61 genes shown are those of the harmonised eQTLGen whole-blood arm that carry a valid S-PrediXcan statistic (24 candidate, 24 non-candidate and 13 T2DM-control genes), corresponding to 61 gene–phenotype pairs in each of DR, DN and DPN (183 in total; gene list and model sizes in Additional file 1: Table S22). This 61-gene arm is a distinct universe from the 69-gene denominator set that supplies the eQTLGen rates of Table 2 and the distributions of Fig. S3 in the main text (27 candidate, 25 non-candidate and 17 T2DM-control genes; 81, 75 and 51 gene–phenotype pairs); the two universes must not be conflated (Methods; Additional file 1: Table S10). Data are from the harmonised eQTLGen recompute (three-way allele harmonisation against FinnGen R13 and 1000G EUR; official eQTLGen FDR < 0.05 models).
