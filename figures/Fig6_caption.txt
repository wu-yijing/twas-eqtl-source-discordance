Fig. 6. eQTL-source enrichment at a fixed nominal threshold across the three diabetic complications. Rates of genes with nominal p < 0.05 are shown for each phenotype (a: diabetic retinopathy; b: diabetic nephropathy; c: diabetic peripheral neuropathy) using GTEx v8 multi-tissue ACAT-O (red) and eQTLGen whole-blood (blue) weights for candidate, non-candidate, T2DM-control and housekeeping genes; error bars are 95% exact Clopper–Pearson confidence intervals, and the numbers above the bars are k/N (genes reaching the nominal threshold / genes tested). Because the BH-corrected rate did not exceed 7.4% in any stratum after the implementation correction, the size-independent fixed nominal threshold is shown as a supplementary endpoint, and no group contrast reaches significance at any of the four thresholds examined (Additional file 1: Tables S7 and S10, the latter giving the group denominators and the two eQTLGen gene universes).
