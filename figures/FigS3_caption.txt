Figure S3. |Z| density distribution for eQTLGen TWAS Z-scores. Kernel density estimates of absolute S-PrediXcan Z-scores from eQTLGen whole-blood weights, stratified by candidate (red), non-candidate (blue) and T2DM control (green) gene groups.
