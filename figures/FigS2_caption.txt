Figure S2. Pull-down vs literature discovery-source stratification. FDR-significant rates under eQTLGen whole-blood weights for candidate genes split by discovery source (RNA pull-down vs literature curation) across DR, DN and DPN. Denominators are the 27 candidate genes with an eQTLGen model (13 pull-down, 14 literature), matching the candidate column of Table 2 in the main text. Error bars are 95% Clopper–Pearson exact confidence intervals; numbers above bars are k/N. FDR q-values were computed by Benjamini–Hochberg correction within each phenotype. The stratification is descriptive only; no between-stratum comparison was performed, because the per-stratum counts are too small (k = 0–1 per phenotype) to support one.
