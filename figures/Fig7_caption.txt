Fig. 7. Cross-cohort replicability of a source-stable finding (RNH1). (a) RNH1 S-PrediXcan Z-scores using GTEx v8 Nerve_Tibial and eQTLGen across DR, DN and DPN (DR: +2.67 versus +2.31). (b) Random-effects meta-analysis of RNH1 in DR using eQTLGen weights in both cohorts, computed with the unmodified official MetaXcan v0.8.1 binary (FinnGen R13 Z = +2.31, N_e = 49,304; UK Biobank GCST90043640 Z = +0.72, N_e = 1,231): pooled mean Z = +1.51, with the error bar on the pooled row drawn at ±1 SE (0.79) and the 95% prediction interval drawn as a horizontal interval on the pooled row (−0.33 to +3.36, spanning zero). Both cohorts are direction-consistent; the UK Biobank estimate rests on 308 cases and is far less precise. With k = 2 cohorts the heterogeneity statistics (Cochran’s Q, I² and τ, which are convention-dependent) and the prediction interval are descriptive only (Additional file 1: Table S4). N_e, effective sample size; PI, prediction interval.
