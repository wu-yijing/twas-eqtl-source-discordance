Fig. 3. Cross-source TWAS Z-score agreement (GTEx v8 Whole_Blood vs eQTLGen). (a) S-PrediXcan Z-scores for the 96 gene–phenotype pairs (32 genes across DR, DN and DPN), with the dashed line marking y = x and, inset, Spearman ρ = 0.39, its gene-cluster bootstrap 95% CI, direction consistency = 68.8% (66/96) and the naive P value for ρ = 8.8 × 10⁻⁵ (anticonservative, because the 96 pairs are clustered within 32 genes). (b) GTEx v8 Whole_Blood Z-score distributions by phenotype, with each complication’s direction consistency above its boxplot. After the implementation correction no pair exceeds |Z| = 2.83 on either axis (maximum |Z_GTEx| = 2.68 and maximum |Z_eQTLGen| = 2.83). Candidate genes are excluded from this arm by design and reach larger |Z| in Fig. 5.
